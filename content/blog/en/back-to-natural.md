## Option one: growing it out with a blur

Every 10 to 12 weeks we blur the regrowth line with a toner half a shade darker than the lengths. It does not shorten the process, but it means you look styled the whole way through rather than neglected. In practice that is usually three visits at 260-320 PLN spread over six months.

This is the choice for people who care about length and do not have a date in the calendar they need to look different for.

## Option two: going dark to the base

Fast, but it requires filling the hair with copper pigment first. Skip that step and the dark colour washes out within two shampoos, leaving a green cast that nothing but another colour service will hide.

One visit, 320-420 PLN, result visible immediately. The downside: for the next few months your natural regrowth will be lighter than the rest - the exact problem we just fixed, in reverse.

## Option three: cutting

The most honest of the three. If the coloured part is the last 15 centimetres and length is not precious to you, scissors solve it in an hour for 180 PLN. No chemistry, no waiting.

## How we decide

At the consultation we check the porosity of the strand and your colour history from the last two years - what, with what, and how often. Only then do we say which route makes sense for you and how long it takes. If one of them is unrealistic, you hear it straight away, not after the first appointment.
